The intersection is the point.

Hi, I'm Daniel Benjamin, a Mechanical Engineering student at UBC and Chassis Lead for UBC Formula Electric.

I enjoy working where mechanical design meets software and control. On the hardware side that means force modelling, FEA-validated structures, and fabrication. On the software side it means analysis pipelines, web apps, and team tooling that turn one-off calculations into systems the whole team can use.
