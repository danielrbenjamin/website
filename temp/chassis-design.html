Chassis Structural Design + Docs // hardware

Owning the 2026 chassis structure end to end — from hand calcs to formal documentation.

ANSYSTorsional StiffnessLaTeXFSAE
UBC Formula Electric · 2026 · chassis lead

As Chassis Lead I own the structural design of the 2026 car's 4130 chromoly tube frame. That spans load-path reasoning, tube sizing, suspension mounting design, and the ANSYS Static Structural work that validates it.

Key analyses include suspension-mounting tube-bending calculations (comparing simply-supported vs fixed-fixed boundary conditions), torsional-stiffness modeling based on the Deakin et al. approach, impact-attenuator sizing, and harness attachment-point analysis under the 30 kN regulatory load.

All of it is backed by formal LaTeX design documents (CHS-F04, SUS-F01) and a maintained design-decision log, so every choice is traceable and defensible in design-event judging.
