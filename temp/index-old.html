01 — Profile

The intersection is the point.

I work where mechanical design meets embedded systems and software. On the hardware side that means FEA-validated structures, suspension kinematics, tire modeling, and fabrication — PCBs, multi-material 3D printing, and DIY CNC. On the software side it means analysis pipelines, web apps, and team tooling that turn one-off calculations into systems the whole team can use.

As Chassis Lead I own the structural design of an FSAE electric race car for the 2026 season — from tube-bending calcs and ANSYS interpretation to the formal design documentation that backs every decision.

What I care about: precision, traceability, and tools that outlive the person who wrote them.
