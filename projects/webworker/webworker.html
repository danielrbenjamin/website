WeBWorKer Chrome Extension

A browser extension with over 500 users that streamlines problem content on WeBWorK, the online homework platform used across a number of university math and science courses.

Problem

WeBWorK is a widely-used online homework system for math and science courses, but its default problem rendering is bare-bones — dense pages, awkward layouts for anyone trying to reference a problem outside the browser, and no easy way to pull a clean copy of a question for notes or a study group. It works, but it doesn't make studying from it pleasant.

How It Works

WeBWorKer injects into the WeBWorK page and restyles problem content for readability, and uses the html2canvas library to let a student capture a clean image of a problem directly from the page — useful for saving a problem to reference later, sharing with a study group, or pulling into personal notes without a screenshot tool fighting the page's original layout.

Adoption

500+
active users

The extension picked up over 500 users, entirely through word of mouth between students looking for a better way to work through WeBWorK problem sets — the kind of organic adoption that tells you the tool was solving a problem people actually had, not one I assumed they had.

Results

What started as a personal fix for my own frustration with WeBWorK's UI turned into a tool other students found and kept using on their own. It's a small extension, but it's a clear example of shipping something for a real, immediate annoyance rather than a hypothetical one.
