UBC MECH Degree Planner

A full-stack planning tool for navigating UBC's Mechanical Engineering degree, built on Next.js and PostgreSQL, with course data scraped from the UBC calendar and historical grade distributions pulled through a ubcgrades.com API proxy.

Problem

UBC's Mechanical Engineering degree has enough prerequisite chains, elective options, and option-specific requirements (like the BMMD stream) that planning a full four years by hand means cross-referencing the calendar, degree navigator, and word of mouth from upper-year students. That process is slow and error-prone, and it's the same problem every single MECH student solves independently, from scratch, every term.

Stack & Data

The planner is a Next.js app backed by PostgreSQL, with course data scraped directly from the UBC calendar rather than hand-entered — so prerequisites, corequisites, and course descriptions stay accurate as the calendar updates instead of drifting out of date the way a manually maintained spreadsheet would.

Grade Distribution Proxy

Beyond just sequencing courses, the planner surfaces historical grade distributions through a proxy in front of ubcgrades.com's API — so a student choosing between electives can see how a course has actually graded in past terms, not just its title and description. That's the difference between "this satisfies the requirement" and "this satisfies the requirement, and here's what past students experienced."

Results

The planner turns a manual, error-prone cross-referencing exercise into a single tool that already knows the prerequisite structure and can show real grade history alongside it — built to answer the actual question a MECH student has every registration period: what should I take, and in what order.
